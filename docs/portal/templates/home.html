Vectorial Boolean functions, reproduced

VBF.jl is a Julia library for vectorial Boolean functions, with a focus on APN functions and the EA and CCZ equivalence relations. It reproduces the computational results of published papers, keeps a catalog of known functions with their provenance, and documents the algorithms it uses.
