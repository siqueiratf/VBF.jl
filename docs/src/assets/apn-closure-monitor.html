About the numbers

A parent completes when its own V5 extensions and switching neighbours are exhausted and every output is admitted or matched to an admitted class. Its descendants can remain queued. The published seeds come first; then the recursive parents follow in admission order, without a profile filter.

Admitted classes are the published seeds plus every new quadratic EA class that the search found. A signature match alone never merges two classes; a match needs a replayed EA witness.

A catalog count appears only when an audit covers the current admitted classes. Confirmed absence proves CCZ separation from the represented catalog; literature-wide novelty remains unresolved.

The result panel counts the direct outputs of the published seeds. The published reference holds published corpora and named functions only. The earlier 111,009 + 79,445 discoveries are a separate dataset.

Timing uses the latest 100 timed completions of the current phase, from first dispatch through expansion and classification. Parallel parents overlap. The rate covers the last five minutes of active search.
