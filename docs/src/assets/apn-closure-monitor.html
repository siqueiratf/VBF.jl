About the numbers

A seed completes when its own V5 extensions and switching neighbours are exhausted. Its descendants can remain queued.

The catalog count combines this campaign and the earlier 190,454-class dataset, deduplicated against a pinned catalog snapshot. Confirmed absence proves CCZ separation from the represented catalog; literature-wide novelty remains unresolved. LUTs awaiting EA admission are excluded.

Timing uses the latest 100 timed completions for the current phase, from first dispatch through expansion and classification. Startup is excluded, saved work is retained, and parallel parents overlap. Throughput covers the last five minutes of active search.
